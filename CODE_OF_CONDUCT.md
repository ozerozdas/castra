# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or the wiki.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@ozerozdas](https://github.com/ozerozdas), through GitHub.
The maintainer may edit or remove content and block participants who do not follow this code. Reports are handled
confidentially.
